# Licenses and compatibility

Verification carried out on 2026-09-08.

## ICCU – Registry of Italian Libraries

* Data license: **CC0 1.0 / public domain**.
* Official source: https://anagrafe.iccu.sbn.it/it/footer/norme-di-utilizzo-dei-dati/
* Attribution: not required by the CC0 license; it remains good practice to cite ICCU as the source.
* Modification: permitted.
* Redistribution: permitted.
* Commercial use: permitted.
* Share-alike: none.
* Separate note: the editorial content of the ICCU website is generally under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IT; this condition does not replace the CC0 specifically declared for the Registry's Open Data.

## ISTAT POSAS 2019 and 2025

* License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**.
* Official source: https://www.istat.it/dati/open-data/
* Attribution: required; cite Istat as the source.
* Modification/adaptation: permitted.
* Redistribution: permitted.
* Commercial use: permitted.
* Share-alike: none.

## Cultural-ON

* License declared in the OWL file: **CC BY 3.0 IT**, URI `http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/it/`.
* Official documentation source: https://dati.beniculturali.it/cultural-ON/ITA.html
* Attribution: required.
* Modification: permitted under the terms of the license.
* Redistribution: permitted.
* Commercial use: permitted.
* Share-alike: none.

## Derived tabular dataset

The pipeline integrates ICCU values under CC0 with Istat demographic data under CC BY 4.0. For the **derived tabular dataset** the documented choice is **CC BY 4.0**, with attribution to Istat and citation of ICCU as the source. This choice does not automatically relicense the ICCU editorial documentation or the Cultural-ON ontology included separately in the repository.
